16th ICCST is over. Looking forward to 17th ICCST
Sept 2025 (updated Oct 2026)

The 16th ICCST was successfully conducted in Halle at the end of August in the Leopoldina, the German National Academy of Sciences in Halle (Saale), Germany. The conference provided a rich selection of inspiring presentations covering vast areas of Selenium and Tellurium chemistry.

The conference honored the legacy of Wolfgang HH Günther, the late founder of the ICCST series who passed away in 2021, by awarding the prestigious Wolfgang Günther Prizes in two categories: Lifetime Achievement and Scientific Excellence in Selenium and Tellurium Chemistry. The awards were presented in the presence of Bernard Günther to:

  • Tristram Chivers (Universtity of Calgary, Canada) for his Lifetime Achievement, and
  • Jens Beckmann (Bremen University, Germany) for Scientific Excellence in Selenium & Tellurium Chemistry
Both recipients received a certificate, a silver medal featuring Wolfgang Günther’s portrait, travel support and a cash prize.

Additional recognition went to outstanding short presentations, with:
  • Koh Sugamata (University of Tsukuba & Rikkyō University, Japan) awarded the first prize for “Selenium- and Tellurium-Centered 2-Heteraallenes,”
  • Svastik Jaiswal (IISER Bhopal, India) receiving the second prize for “Directing Group Strategy for the Isolation of Organoselenium(VI) Benzoselenonates: Metal-Free Catalysts for Hydrogen Evolution Reaction,” and
  • Wei Cao (Beijing Normal University, China) securing the third prize for “Selenium-Modified Melanins and Their Radiation-Protective Properties In Vivo.”

Ignacio Vargas-Baca, a member of the ICCST International Advisory Board and affiliated with McMaster University (Hamilton, Canada), announced the upcoming 17th ICCST to be hosted in Hamilton, Canada, from August 7 to 11, 2028, inviting the global scientific community to join this event.
