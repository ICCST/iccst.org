Obituary
Nov 2026

Unfortunately, our colleague and scientific friend Professor Leopold Flohé (1938-2026) passed away. His pioneering work has left an enduring legacy in enzymology and in the biology and chemistry of selenium.

A graduate of the University of Tübingen in medicine and biochemistry, he held professorships at Tübingen, Aachen and Braunschweig, in addition to assuming prominent roles within the pharmaceutical industry. His seminal 1973 discovery that glutathione peroxidase is a selenium-containing enzyme led to the identification of the first mammalian selenoprotein, thereby inaugurating a novel field of research in oxidative metabolism and redox regulation. He was an active member of numerous learned societies and served as Editor-in-Chief of BioFactors and as an editorial board member of Biological Chemistry. Professor Flohé contributed to the 3rd ICCST in Metz, France, in 1979, serving on its Organizing Committee, and he remained a long-standing member of our International Scientific Advisory Board. We mourn his loss with profound sadness and remember with gratitude his lasting contributions to the scientific community.
